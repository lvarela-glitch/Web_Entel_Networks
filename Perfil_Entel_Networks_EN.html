03 / Assessment and continuity

Resilience and risk readiness

One design connects security, network and continuity.

  • Security audit. Review of architecture, infrastructure and addressing; gap analysis against the agreed regulation or standard.
  • Resilient networks. Design of fault-tolerant LAN and routing, link redundancy and high availability.
  • Continuity and recovery. Support for business continuity and disaster recovery strategies aligned with client objectives.
  • Consulting and support. Escalation and review services for network and security infrastructure, according to the contracted coverage.

04 / Networks & connectivity

Resilient architectures and available applications

We design, assess and integrate enterprise networks for continuity, visibility and performance, from LANs and branches to application services.

Alternate route during a link outage.

Fault-tolerant LAN

Redundant links, devices and paths help maintain connectivity when a failure occurs.

Failover routing

Alternate routes and devices detect interruptions and keep traffic moving over an available path.

High availability

Architectures that detect, recover from and maintain service through device, link or cabling failures.

Design accounts for providers, routing equipment and available paths between sites, based on the client environment.

05 / Enterprise switching

Access, distribution, core and management

Integration of devices, sites and applications with room to grow.

Access

L2/L3 switches for campuses and branches. PoE, PoE+ and PoE++ options for users, IP phones, Wi-Fi 6/6E, PTZ cameras and IoT, with segmentation and per-port control.

Distribution and aggregation

10/25 Gb links, redundancy between layers, converged voice, data and collaboration, and continuity between access and core.

Core

Low-latency L2/L3 switching and 40/100/400 Gb links, based on the campus or data center design.

Multisite management

Multivendor visibility, threshold and trend alerts, and related information to identify operational issues.

06 / Assessment, performance and support

From assessment to operation

Network architecture and IP addressing

Review of topology, segmentation, IP plans, routes and dependencies between sites and services.

Network and security audit

Gap analysis against the standards or regulations selected for the project.

Load balancing and acceleration

Link optimization and request distribution across servers to reduce bottlenecks.

Application and link options

Point to point

Synchronous optimization at both ends; may include compression, deduplication and caching.

Web portals

Asynchronous delivery optimization that accounts for the transmission and reception characteristics of each connection.

Servers

Request balancing across data and application servers to use capacity efficiently and keep services available.

Service lifecycle: design and sizing · implementation and launch · operational and corrective support · post-deployment assistance.

Consulting and escalation: IT and network infrastructure support; security infrastructure support; infrastructure and security audits.
